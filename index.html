About Us

At Sasha Nails & Beauty, we believe that beautiful nails are more than just a service—they are a statement. Our studio brings international trends to Johannesburg, combining technical excellence with a passion for artistry.

Established: 2015

Location: Johannesburg Ranburg square, South Africa

Speciality: Acrylic Sculpture, Gel Extensions, Nail Art & hair Styling

Our Promise: We use only premium, industry-leading products to ensure long-lasting results and the health of your natural nails.

Whether you are preparing for a special occasion or simply treating yourself, our skilled technicians are here to ensure you leave feeling confident and polished.
